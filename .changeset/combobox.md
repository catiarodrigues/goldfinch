New `Combobox`: a text input that filters a list of options as you type, for lists too long to scroll, such as countries or time zones. It takes `items` as strings or `{ value, label }` objects, works with `label`, `description` and `error` like the other form controls, and is built on Base UI's Combobox for keyboard and screen reader support. Import it from the barrel or from `@catiarodrigues/goldfinch/components/combobox`.
